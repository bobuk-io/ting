# Ting 0.3.0

## Highlights

- **launcha** joins **selecta**, the leader-key menu. Type part of an app name
  in launcha and press `Return` to open it or bring a running app to the front.
- **Three ways to open launcha:** double-tap the leader at the top level of
  selecta, set a separate global hotkey in Settings, or choose **Open launcha**
  in the menu-bar menu. The separate hotkey is unset by default and must differ
  from the leader. Double-tap is on by default, with a 0.3 s interval adjustable
  from 0.1 to 1 s. The first press still opens selecta at once; an intervening
  sequence key cancels the double tap, and holding the leader does not count.
- **Name search** ignores case and accents and supports word starts, initials
  (`vsc` → Visual Studio Code), substrings and text typed with the wrong Russian
  or Ukrainian keyboard layout (`ыфафкш` → Safari). An empty search lists apps.
- **Usage ranking:** matching apps rank by successful opens through selecta and
  launcha in the last 7 days, then most recent launch, match quality and name.
  Failed opens and Finder reveals do not count. Ting does not watch apps opened
  in other ways.
- **Spotlight fallback:** only when no app matches a non-empty search, launcha
  asks Spotlight for files in your home folder. It excludes `~/Library`, hidden
  folders and app bundles. Files open with their usual app.
- **Folder consent:** Desktop, Documents and Downloads are skipped until you
  allow access in launcha or Settings. macOS may then ask for access to each
  folder. Allowing access also adds apps on your Desktop to the app list. You
  can turn this access off in Settings.
- **Panel controls:** the leader now appears as a tuning-fork symbol, with your
  configured key in its tooltip. **Show panel title** controls the
  `Ting · selecta` and `Ting · launcha` title rows. It is on by default; hiding
  the titles leaves the leader symbol, sequence and search input visible.

## Using launcha

The native Mac search field supports text editing, paste and input methods.
Use `↑` and `↓` to choose a result, `Return` to open it, `⌘Return` to show it in
Finder, and `Esc` to close. Clicking elsewhere or switching Spaces also closes
launcha.

Ting scans `/Applications`, `/System/Applications` and `~/Applications` at
startup and each time launcha opens. It opens the exact app bundle found.
While the panel stays open, Ting checks every 15 seconds whether a refresh is
due; these background scans run at most once per minute. The one-minute limit
does not apply to startup or panel-opening scans.

Launch history and folder consent stay on your Mac, separate from
`~/.config/ting/config.json`, in
`~/Library/Application Support/Ting/launcha-history.json` and
`~/Library/Application Support/Ting/launcha-consent.json`. With `TING_CONFIG`
set, each file sits next to that config and uses its name as a prefix; for
example, `/tmp/ting-test.json.launcha-history.json` and
`/tmp/ting-test.json.launcha-consent.json`.

## Upgrade and install

Requires **macOS 14 or later**. The universal ZIP supports **Apple Silicon and Intel**.

If Ting 0.2.0 or 0.2.1 is installed, choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu-bar
menu. Your settings, leader and bindings are kept. Older configuration files
get the new defaults: leader double-tap enabled, no separate launcha hotkey and
panel titles shown. Protected folders still need your consent.

For a new or manual installation, download `Ting-0.3.0-universal.zip` from
[Releases](https://github.com/bobuk-io/ting/releases/latest) and unzip it. Quit
any running copy of Ting, move `Ting.app` to `/Applications` (replace the old
copy for a manual upgrade), and open it. Ting lives in the menu bar and has no
Dock icon. On first launch, choose a leader key; there is no default leader.

The official release is signed with Developer ID, notarized by Apple, and
includes a stapled notarization ticket. Verify the archive with
`SHA256SUMS.txt` if desired. Ting needs no Accessibility or Input Monitoring
permission.
